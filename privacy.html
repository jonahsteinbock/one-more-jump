Privacy & advertising

One More Jump does not require an account. Your personal best is saved only in your browser's local storage. The game does not send your score to a server.

There are currently no advertisements or advertising tracking scripts in this game. The reserved advertising area is only a labeled placeholder, not an ad.

If advertisements are introduced later, this notice must be updated before they are enabled to describe the provider, cookies, data collection, consent choices, and applicable privacy rights. Hosting providers may process standard technical access logs under their own policies.
